Blue return special deduction: ¥650,000 vs ¥550,000 vs ¥100,000 in actual tax

A “blue return” (青色申告, aoiro shinkoku) is the return type for business owners who keep proper books and have the tax office's approval. It comes with a special deduction of ¥100,000, ¥550,000 or ¥650,000 off business income; everyone else files a “white return” with none. The gap between ¥650,000 and ¥550,000 is ¥100,000 times your tax rate, but the rate depends on your income and the deduction itself can change your bracket. About this result

This is an estimate based on the figures you entered and simplified assumptions. Your actual tax depends on what you file and on your municipality. It does not replace the judgment of a tax accountant (zeirishi), the tax office or your city office. Check with a professional or the office in charge before any important decision.

It assumes you are a resident of Japan for tax purposes for the whole year and still live in Japan on January 1 next year. Foreign income, tax treaties and the rules for non-residents are not handled.

How it is calculated, and the basis

Income tax and resident tax are calculated at each of the four levels and compared.

Difference in tax ≈ difference in deduction × (income tax rate × 1.021 + resident tax 10%)

  • The special deduction for blue returns takes ¥550,000 (¥650,000 if further conditions are met) or ¥100,000 off your income, depending on how you keep books and file (NTA Tax Answer No. 2072: special deduction for blue returns (Japanese only)). The NTA's English income tax guide uses the same terms, “blue return” and “white return”.
  • ¥550,000 requires double-entry bookkeeping for business income, a balance sheet and profit-and-loss statement attached, and a return filed by the deadline. ¥650,000 requires, on top of that, filing online with e-Tax or keeping qualifying electronic books. Blue-return filers who meet neither get ¥100,000 (NTA Tax Answer No. 2072: special deduction for blue returns (Japanese only)).
  • The deduction cannot exceed your profit. If business income is below the deduction, only the income itself is deducted (NTA Tax Answer No. 2072: special deduction for blue returns (Japanese only)).
  • The income tax rate is 5% to 45% depending on taxable income (NTA Tax Answer No. 2260: income tax rates (Japanese only)); the income-based part of resident tax is a flat 10% (Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications: individual resident tax (Japanese only)).
  • This page is for tax year 2026. From tax year 2027 the rules change: ¥650,000 for double-entry books filed with e-Tax, ¥750,000 with certain electronic bookkeeping on top (automatic links to invoice data, a record of corrections and deletions, and so on), and ¥100,000 for double-entry books filed on paper. The ¥100,000 deduction for simple books is limited to people whose business revenue two years earlier was ¥10 million or less, among others (Ministry of Finance: FY2026 tax reform materials (tax administration) (Japanese only); NTA: changes to the special deduction for blue returns from tax year 2027 (Japanese only)).
  • The calculator shows the difference in tax only. It does not check whether you meet the conditions for a level.

Common questions

How much is the difference between ¥650,000 and ¥550,000 in tax?

The deductions differ by ¥100,000; the tax difference depends on your income. With an income of ¥5,000,000 (same assumptions as the table), going from ¥550,000 to ¥650,000 cuts income tax and resident tax by ¥20,200 a year in total. The higher your income tax rate, the larger the gap.

How much do I save by moving from a white return to a ¥650,000 blue return?

With an income of ¥5,000,000 (same assumptions as the table), ¥197,700 a year (¥30,400 at the ¥100,000 level, ¥177,500 at ¥550,000). To file a blue return you must submit the application (青色申告承認申請書) to the tax office by March 15 of the year you want it to start, or within two months of opening if you started the business on or after January 16 (NTA Tax Answer No. 2070: the blue return system (Japanese only)). You cannot switch that year's return from white to blue at year-end.

Does the blue-return deduction also lower National Health Insurance?

Yes. The income-based part of the premium is charged on total income minus the basic deduction, and the blue-return deduction lowers total income (Order for Enforcement of the National Health Insurance Act, Art. 29-7 (Japanese only); Local Tax Act, Art. 703-4 (Japanese only)). That is different from iDeCo and furusato nozei. Rates differ by municipality, so enter yours to see a rough difference (the annual cap is not applied).

Is the deduction changing from tax year 2027?

Yes. From tax year 2027, double-entry books filed with e-Tax give ¥650,000, and keeping electronic books that meet certain conditions on top gives ¥750,000. Double-entry books filed on paper give ¥100,000. The ¥100,000 deduction for simple books is limited to people whose business revenue two years earlier was ¥10 million or less, among others (Ministry of Finance: FY2026 tax reform materials (tax administration) (Japanese only); NTA: changes to the special deduction for blue returns from tax year 2027 (Japanese only)). This page calculates tax year 2026, with the ¥550,000 and ¥650,000 levels.

In a low-income year, can I deduct the full ¥650,000?

Only up to your business profit. If revenue minus expenses is ¥400,000, you deduct ¥400,000 even if you meet the conditions for ¥650,000 (NTA Tax Answer No. 2072: special deduction for blue returns (Japanese only)). The calculator applies this cap.
